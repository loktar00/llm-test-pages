01 · Palette

Tuned, not inverted.

Night isn't the day with its lights off. Daylight sits on cool paper with a single warm metal; nightfall sinks into harbor blue, and the ink warms up to match the moon. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Daybreak 06:41
Nightfall 21:05

Two weather systems, one set of variables.

Every color the page touches lives behind a custom property. When the switch flips, backgrounds, cards, ink, borders and shadows all ease across together in 550 ms — while the painted sky cross-fades in opacity, because gradients refuse to interpolate politely.

Contrast is held in both weathers: body text stays above WCAG AA against its surface, and the gold accent simply changes temperature — the sun's metal becomes the moon's.

"Gold is the one thread that runs through both skies — continuity is what makes the morph feel like time passing."

/* solstice — two tuned palettes */

:root {
  --bg:     #F1F4F7;  /* cool paper    */
  --ink:    #16283C;  /* harbor slate  */
  --card:   #FFFFFF;
  --accent: #D98E2B;  /* sun gold      */
}

[data-theme="dark"] {
  --bg:     #0E1729;  /* midnight      */
  --ink:    #EAE6DA;  /* moon-warm ink */
  --card:   #15213A;
  --accent: #F2C86B;  /* moon gold     */
}

Press T anywhere — the sky follows. Your choice is remembered on the next visit, and on the first one the page simply asks the operating system what time it feels like.

02 · The morph

How a sun becomes a moon.

No icon swap, no cross-fade between two pictures. One drawing, four choreographed moves — try the instrument below, it's wired to the same sky.

01

Rays retract 500 MS · SPRING · 30 MS STAGGER

Eight rays scale to zero toward the hub, each 30 ms behind its neighbor, while the whole crown counter-rotates 80° — the sun visibly pulls its light in.

02

The bite slides in 550 MS · EASE

A disc the exact color of the knob eases over the core's upper right, carving a crescent out of the same circle that was the sun a moment ago.

03

Craters settle 220 MS DELAY · OVERSHOOT

Three craters fade and scale up with a slight overshoot, landing only after the crescent has formed — geology arrives after astronomy.

04

The palette follows 550 MS · EVERYTHING

Every surface, ink, border and shadow eases across in lockstep. Gradients cross-fade in opacity, and a first-paint guard means the very first load never flashes.
