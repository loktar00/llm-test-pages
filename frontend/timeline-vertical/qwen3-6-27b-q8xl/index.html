Our Journey

A decade of innovation, growth, and redefining the industry standard.

2014

The Spark

Founded in a small garage in San Francisco, three developers set out to solve the complexity of cloud infrastructure.

2015

First Product Launch

We released "Nebula Core," our flagship automation tool. It gained 1,000 active users within the first week of the beta.

2017

Series A Funding

Recognizing our potential, top-tier VCs invested $12M to help us scale. We moved into our first real office downtown.

2019

Global Expansion

Opened offices in London and Singapore. Our team grew from 15 to over 100 employees across three continents.

2021

The Pivot

Adapting to the remote-first world, we completely reimagined our collaboration suite, doubling our enterprise client base.

2023

Market Leader

Named "Best Infrastructure Tool" by TechDaily. We are now powering the backend for over 50% of the Fortune 100.

2024

The Next Chapter

Launching our AI-driven predictive analytics engine. The journey continues as we push the boundaries of what's possible.
