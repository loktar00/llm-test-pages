Our Journey

From a small garage idea to a global innovation hub. Explore our milestones.

2010

The Origin Story

Founded in a small garage in Seattle, three developers had a vision to revolutionize cloud computing with a simple, scalable architecture.

2013

Series A Funding

Secured $5M in seed funding, allowing us to hire our first dedicated design team and expand into a dedicated office space downtown.

2015

Global Expansion

Opened our first international office in London. Our user base crossed the 1 million mark, requiring a complete overhaul of our server infrastructure.

2018

The AI Pivot

Launched "Nexus," our proprietary machine learning engine. This product became our flagship offering, integrating predictive analytics for enterprise clients.

2020

Remote First Culture

We embraced a fully remote workforce before it became an industry standard. Productivity soared, and talent acquisition became borderless.

2022

IPO Success

Successfully listed on the NASDAQ. The company valuation hit $2B, marking our transition from a startup to a major tech player.

2024

Sustainability Initiative

Achieved carbon neutrality across all global operations. We are now committed to net-zero emissions by 2030 through renewable energy investments.
