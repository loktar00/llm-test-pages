Milestones

The moments that defined us

2009

The Garage Beginning

Two college dropouts, a whiteboard covered in diagrams, and a conviction that software could be simpler. We wrote our first line of code in a cramped garage in Palo Alto, fueled by cold brew and ambition.

2011

First Seed Round

$1.2M from a visionary angel investor who believed in our mission. We moved into a real office — a converted warehouse with exposed brick and a coffee machine that never stopped running.

2013

Product Launch — Version 1.0

After 18 months of relentless development, we shipped. 10,000 signups in the first week. The servers crashed three times that day. We fixed them all by sunrise.

2015

Series B & Global Expansion

$28M raised. Offices opened in London and Tokyo. Our team grew from 30 to 200 overnight. The culture stayed the same — curious, humble, obsessed with craft.

2017

One Million Users

A milestone we celebrated with pizza, confetti, and a heartfelt letter from our CEO. Our NPS hit 72. We proved that growth and quality aren't mutually exclusive.

2019

The Platform Pivot

We opened our API to third-party developers. An ecosystem was born. Within a year, 500+ integrations were live. Our product became the foundation for thousands of other products.

2021

Weathering the Storm

The global pandemic tested us. We pivoted to remote-first, invested in mental health, and emerged stronger. Revenue grew 40% year-over-year. Our people came first — always.

2024

The Next Chapter

2,000+ team members across 12 countries. AI-native products. A commitment to carbon neutrality by 2026. The garage is long gone, but the spirit remains — build boldly, ship fast, care deeply.
