Creamy Tomato Basil Soup

A velvety classic with fresh herbs & a hint of garlic

35 min Easy 4 servings
Servings
4

0 of 8 steps completed

Ingredients

  • Canned whole tomatoes 28 oz
  • Heavy cream ½ cup
  • Fresh basil leaves 1 cup
  • Garlic cloves, minced 4 cloves
  • Yellow onion, diced 1 large
  • Vegetable broth 1 cup
  • Butter 2 tbsp
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat butter and olive oil in a large pot over medium heat until the butter melts and begins to foam.

  2. Add the diced onion and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and translucent.

  3. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until fragrant — don't let it brown.

  4. Pour in the canned tomatoes with their juices and the vegetable broth. Bring to a gentle simmer.

  5. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.

  6. Remove from heat and stir in the fresh basil leaves. Let them wilt into the soup for 2 minutes.

  7. Carefully transfer the soup to a blender and blend until smooth. Work in batches if needed, venting the lid.

  8. Return the soup to the pot, stir in the heavy cream, and season with salt and pepper. Serve hot with crusty bread.
