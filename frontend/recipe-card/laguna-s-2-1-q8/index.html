Avocado Quinoa Bowl

20 min
4 servings
Easy
4

Ingredients

Instructions

  • 1
    Cook quinoa in vegetable broth for 15 minutes until fluffy. Let cool slightly.
  • 2
    Dice avocado and mince red onion. Combine in a bowl with lime juice.
  • 3
    Fluff quinoa with a fork and fold into avocado mixture gently.
  • 4
    Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt, and toss to combine.
