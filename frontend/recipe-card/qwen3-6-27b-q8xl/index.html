Rustic Basil Pesto Pasta

⏱ 20 min
🍽 4 servings
📊 Easy
Adjust Servings:
4 servings
Ingredients
  • 400g Spaghetti
  • 2 cups Fresh Basil
  • 0.5 cup Pine Nuts
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 0.66 cup Parmesan Cheese
  • 0.5 cup Olive Oil
Instructions
  • 1
    Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions until al dente.
  • 2
    While pasta cooks, toast pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium heat until golden brown. Watch carefully so they don't burn.
  • 3
    In a food processor, blend basil, garlic, and toasted pine nuts until finely chopped.
  • 4
    With the motor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
  • 5
    Stir in the parmesan cheese. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  • 6
    Toss the drained pasta with the pesto sauce. Serve immediately with extra parmesan on top.
