Our Journey

Building the Future,
One Milestone at a Time

From a two-person garage operation to a global force for innovation — explore the moments that defined us.

1998

The Spark in a Garage

Two college dropouts with a shared vision and a second-hand PC laid the first line of code. No business plan, no investors — just stubborn belief that software could change how people connect.

2001

First Office, First Hire

A cramped above-a-restaurant space in downtown Seattle became our first real office. We hired our 10th employee — a designer who would later become our CTO — and adopted our first real coffee machine.

2004

Product Launch: Nexus Platform

After two years of late nights and instant noodles, Nexus launched to a quiet but immediate response. Enterprise clients signed on within the first week. Revenue hit $1M ARR by quarter's end.

2007

Series B: $18M Raised

Leading VCs recognized what we saw early — the platform was infrastructure-grade. The funding round allowed us to double the engineering team and open our London satellite office.

2010

The Great Pivot

We killed our most profitable product to build something nobody asked for yet — a cloud-native architecture. It was terrifying. It was right. Competitors who laughed were the ones who couldn't catch up.

2013

Global Expansion: 12 Offices

From São Paulo to Singapore, we opened regional hubs. Our 500-person team spoke 23 languages. The org chart looked like a world map, and the culture stayed remarkably cohesive.

2016

IPO Day: $4.2B Valuation

The bell rang at the NYSE. Our founders, still wearing the same hoodies, watched from a balcony in Manhattan. The stock jumped 34% on day one. We celebrated with terrible catered pizza.

2019

Acquisition of Meridian Labs

We acquired our boldest competitor not to eliminate them — but to absorb their genius. 200 engineers joined overnight, and their AI research division became our most innovative team within a year.

2021

Open Source Initiative

We released 40% of our core platform as open source. The developer community responded with 12,000 GitHub stars in a week. Trust, it turns out, is the best growth strategy.

2024

The Next Chapter: AI-First

Every product line now has AI at its core. We're no longer a software company that uses AI — we're an intelligence company that builds software. The garage days feel like a different lifetime, and the mission is clearer than ever.

26 years and counting. The best is yet to come.
