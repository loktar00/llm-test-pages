2010

Founded with Vision

Our journey began in a small garage with three engineers who believed in building something exceptional. We launched our first product, a lightweight analytics tool for startups.

2013

Series A Funding

We secured $5M in Series A funding led by Northbridge Ventures. This milestone allowed us to expand our team from 8 to 40 and launch our enterprise-tier platform.

2016

Global Expansion

We opened offices in London and Singapore, localizing our platform for five new languages. Revenue grew 300% year-over-year, and we welcomed our 100th employee.

2019

AI Integration Launch

We introduced AI-powered predictive analytics to our platform. The feature drove a 60% increase in customer retention and earned us the Tech Innovator Award from Industry Leaders Association.

2022

Public Listing

We went public on NASDAQ under the ticker CMPT. The IPO raised $120M, funding our next phase of innovation and sustainability initiatives across all global operations.

2024

Carbon Neutral Achievement

We became fully carbon neutral across all offices and data centers. Over 500 team members now power products trusted by 2M+ users worldwide, with a mission to innovate responsibly.
